## English

I'm an Artificial Intelligence undergraduate at **PUC-Rio** and a **Fundação Behring** scholar. I like hard problems, I start moving even before I have every piece of information, and I bring in the right people so the solution takes shape.

What I care about is getting AI out of the prototype and into real use. In practice that has meant:

- **Building a tech function from scratch** at Instituto 12, a community and coworking institute in Rio, where I'm the technology and AI person: the members hub, the automations and the playbook so the work outlives me.
- **Delivering AI solutions** at Convem, a fintech, including an internal knowledge-assessment platform that is live today.
- **Turning hackathons into products:** 1st place at Anthropic's Claude Impact Lab (Rio daycare access, now being implemented with the city) and 3rd place at Visagio's Hackathon VX (Pactua, an AI agent that negotiates debt by listening before it offers).
- **Designing the logic behind products,** like the Overload metric and task-generation rules of AntiKaos, an iPhone app on the App Store.
- **Building community:** I founded the Applied AI League (LIA) and co-founded the AI Student Association at PUC-Rio, and I volunteer teaching physics to kids.

Before AI, there was math: years of olympiad training at **IMPA**, 15+ gold medals (OBMEP, OBA, ONC, Kangaroo) and the 2022 LigMAT championship. I also studied Data Science & AI at FGV and spent six months at the **Apple Developer Academy**.
